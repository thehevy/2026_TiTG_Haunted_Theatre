1-16 characters; shown in the heartbeat so you can tell boards apart.
Used when no D8/D9 jumper is installed. Jumpers override it: D8 = 5 s, D9 = 10 s, both = 15 s.
Uses the same duration, lockout, and re-arm gap as an IR shot.
IR shots play 01/001, 01/002, ... in order. After this long with no IR sound the next shot starts at 001 again (0 = never). D2 plays 02/001, D10 plays 02/002.
Triggers during a pulse and this gap are ignored.
